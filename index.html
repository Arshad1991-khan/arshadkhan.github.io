About

Academic profile

I am a Senior Lecturer in Software Engineering and Information Technology at Yoobee Colleges in Auckland, New Zealand. My work combines postgraduate teaching, research supervision and applied research in emerging digital technologies.

I completed my PhD at Auckland University of Technology in 2023. My doctoral research focused on a novel blockchain-based incentive mechanism to mitigate inequalities in prescription management systems.

My research interests span blockchain applications, AI and machine learning, healthcare and digital health, cybersecurity, cloud security, smart systems, supply-chain security, privacy and data governance, and secure information systems.

I also bring international academic experience, including teaching and research activity in China.
