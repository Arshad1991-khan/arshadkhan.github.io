Biography

Dr Arshad Khan is a Senior Lecturer, Technology at Yoobee Colleges of Creative Innovation, Auckland, New Zealand, where he coordinates master’s-level courses in Blockchain Technology, Software Engineering, Data Analytics and AI/Machine Learning. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Auckland University of Technology (2018–2023), following an MSc in Computer Science from Qurtuba University of Science and Technology and a BIT from Agriculture University of Science and Technology, Peshawar. His research centres on financial technology — blockchain and distributed ledger systems, cryptocurrency infrastructure, smart contracts and decentralised finance — alongside applied analytics, machine learning and privacy-preserving data systems.

He currently holds a Visiting Post-Doctoral appointment at University Canada West and recently completed a Post-Doctoral Researcher role at Lincoln College University, both centred on blockchain, AI and data governance. His PhD research was conducted in partnership with Orion Health – Precision Driven Health, and he spent a year as a researcher and teaching assistant at Shaanxi Normal University, Xi’an, China, on a Chinese Government Scholarship. He supervises 25+ master’s applied research projects, serves as an External Examiner for postgraduate theses, and reviews for IEEE, Springer, Wiley, Elsevier and MDPI journals.
